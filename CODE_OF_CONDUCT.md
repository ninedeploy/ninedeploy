# Code of conduct

NineDeploy follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, keep discussions about the work, and do not harass anyone. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and every other project space.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**, which only maintainers can read), and say that it is a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainers may remove comments or contributions, or block participants, who break this code.
